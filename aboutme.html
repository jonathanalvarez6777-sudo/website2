My name is Jonathan Alvarez, I am an artist that creates through many different mediums. I have painted, drew, sculpted, and created art through graphic design. I have no memories where I wasn't an artist, I always loved to draw and paint and I was always naturally good at it. A big theme in some of the art I create explores the human body and seeing it in its artistic form. I want to create art that pushes the limits to what art can be.

I want to have my name written into the history of art. I want to continue to learn different forms of art, explore and spread what I feel art is, art is everywhere. In some ways it feels like being an artist is gods gift to showing us what its like to be him, to be able to create a world of your own, the ability to create. I want to create as much as I can.
